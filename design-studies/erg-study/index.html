About Us

More than a recruiting agency. A division of Express Network.

Express Recruiting Group was created to bring Express Network's decades of legal industry experience to a new service: legal talent acquisition. We understand law firm operations because our parent organization has served law firms nationwide since 1985 — we're not an outside agency learning the industry, we're already part of it.

We work with national, regional, plaintiff-side, and defense law firms, recruiting everyone from attorneys to legal support and operations staff, with a personalized approach every step of the way.
