The process

What actually happens, step by step.

  1. Step 1

    Consultation

    We look at your history, your status and your options together. You leave knowing what is possible and what it costs.

  2. Step 2

    Preparing the petition

    We gather evidence, complete the forms and check them twice. Most denials we see are avoidable paperwork errors.

  3. Step 3

    Filing and waiting

    We file, track the receipt notices and tell you what each one means. You will not have to guess what a letter says.

  4. Step 4

    Interview or hearing

    We prepare you for the questions and we go with you. Nobody from this office sends a client in alone.

  5. Step 5

    After the decision

    Approved, we help with next steps. Denied, we explain the appeal or the alternative route the same week.

Before your consultation

Bring what you have. Missing documents are normal.

Nobody arrives with a complete file. Bring what you can find and we will work out the rest together.

  • Passport or national ID, current or expired
  • Any notice you have received from USCIS or the court
  • Birth and marriage certificates, if you have them
  • Records of time in the United States
  • Any prior immigration paperwork, even if incomplete
  • A list of your questions — there are no bad ones
